AutoClip Studio

A desktop app that turns your own gaming videos into vertical clips with subtitles.

What it does

  • Imports your own gameplay recordings, or videos you are authorized to use.
  • Detects highlight moments from audio, scene changes and speech, with a relevance score for each one.
  • Renders vertical 1080×1920 clips with smart cropping and dynamic subtitles (original language, French, English, or both).
  • Lets you review, edit and approve every clip before it is published.

Posting to TikTok

  1. Connect your TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit. Your password is never entered in the app.
  2. For each approved clip, choose who can see it, the comment/duet/stitch settings and the caption, and confirm TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation.
  3. Post now or schedule the clip. The app uploads it with TikTok's official Content Posting API and shows the post status. You can also send a clip as a draft to finish posting in the TikTok app.

Privacy

AutoClip Studio runs on your computer. Your videos, settings and TikTok tokens stay on your device and are never sent to the developer. Read the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
