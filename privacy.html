AutoClip Studio — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

AutoClip Studio ("the App") is a desktop application that runs entirely on the user's own computer. It helps a creator turn their own videos into short vertical clips and, at the creator's request, post them to their own TikTok account through TikTok's official APIs.

1. Data we collect

The App has no servers of its own. The developer does not receive, collect or store any of your personal data. Everything the App handles stays on your computer:

  • Your videos and clips, which you import, edit and render locally.
  • TikTok account data, only after you connect your account with TikTok Login Kit: an access token and a refresh token, your TikTok open ID, and the basic profile information returned by TikTok (nickname, username, avatar URL) together with your posting settings (available privacy levels, interaction settings, maximum video duration).
  • App settings, which you enter yourself.

2. How we use TikTok data

  • Your profile information is shown in the App so you know which account a video will be posted to.
  • Your tokens are used only to upload and publish the videos that you explicitly choose to post or schedule, and to check the status of those posts.
  • The App never posts without your action: for each video you choose the privacy level, interaction settings and caption, and you confirm TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation.

3. Storage and security

Tokens and settings are stored in a local database on your computer (data/app.db). They are never sent to the developer or to any third party other than TikTok. You can disconnect at any time from the App (Settings › TikTok account › Disconnect). This revokes the token with TikTok and deletes it locally. You can also remove the App's access from your TikTok account settings.

4. Third-party services

  • TikTok (Login Kit, Content Posting API): used to connect your account and post your videos, subject to TikTok's own privacy policy.
  • Optional features that you can choose to enable, each subject to its provider's policy: downloading speech-recognition and translation models (Hugging Face, Argos Translate), importing a video from a link you provide, and AI text generation (Ollama on your computer, or Anthropic's API with your own key).

5. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data. No analytics or advertising trackers are included in the App.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your computer until you delete it. Deleting the App's data folder removes all locally stored data, including TikTok tokens.

7. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required by TikTok in your country).

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.

9. Contact

For any question about this policy, open an issue on the GitHub repository that hosts this website.
